About me

I’m a product designer with over 10 years of experience designing complex, high-impact digital products that support people in managing finances, information, tasks, and everyday workflows. My background spans large-scale banking applications used by millions, secure communication tools, and consumer services – environments where clarity, trust, and efficiency are critical.

My core strengths

Fintech & B2B SaaS

I have led design for large-scale mobile banking products, including the app of Commerzbank, one of Germany’s leading banks and Access Digital, a B2B banking platform serving 400+ financial institutions and over 3 million users in the US.

Video communication & Messaging

I’ve worked on features like video chat and chatbots in banking apps, and designed secure messaging experiences for AnyTalk Messenger across mobile and desktop platforms. I also created an Appointment Manager enabling video, phone, and on-site meetings that improved customer support efficiency and reduced operational costs.

Planning tools

I designed various features that help people manage appointments, services, and ongoing responsibilities. My experience includes calendar and scheduling experiences, coordination flows, and systems that make complex timelines and constraints feel simple and manageable. I focus on clarity, cognitive load reduction, and giving users better control over their time and commitments.

Pettech & consumer product experience

I have hands-on experience in “pettech” through designing the Dog Walk mobile app for dog walkers, covering the entire journey from ideation to research and delivery.

Design systems & cross-platform craft

I have created and expanded cross-platform design systems, refreshed visual styles, and updated key app features with new components for consistency and scalability.

Product discovery, UX research & testing

I enjoy and actively practice discovery, UX research, usability and unmoderated testing, and iterative validation to shape clear problem definitions and effective solutions.
