About me

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 10+ years of experience, currently at Neugelb Studios working on Commerzbank’s digital banking services. I’ve worked across startups and large companies, consumer and B2B products, and teams of very different sizes.

How I got here

My path into product design started outside of digital products. I’ve always been drawn to visual culture and street art, so studying graphic design felt like a natural step. After university I designed posters, then joined a game studio, where I took my first steps in UI design, creating interfaces, icons and illustrations for online games. That work got me interested in how people use digital products, and I moved on to designing mobile, desktop and web apps. That visual background still shapes how I work today.
